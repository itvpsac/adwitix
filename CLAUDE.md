# Working agreement (from the project owner)

- **Do not deploy unless explicitly asked.** A merge to `main` of
  itvpsac/adwitix-backend deploys the backend (Deploy backend workflow);
  a merge to `main` of itvpsac/adwitix deploys the frontend (Vercel). So
  commit and push work to the working branch only, and do not open/merge
  PRs into `main` until a deploy is requested.
- **Batch deploys:** at most once a day, with the day's work pushed
  together, when asked.
- **Daily review at 9:00 AM IST:** an audit of work done and work
  pending. Keep a clear done/pending list ready for it.
